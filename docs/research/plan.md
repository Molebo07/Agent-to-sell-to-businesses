# Research plan

## Objectives

- Understand the operational pain of customer chat management for South African SMEs.
- Validate demand for WhatsApp + website AI support and lead capture.
- Identify trust points, handoff triggers, and willingness to pay.

## Participants

- 8 interviews with SMB owners or staff in sectors such as retail, hospitality, clinics, salons, real estate, and service businesses.
- 3 interviews with people who currently answer WhatsApp messages manually.
- 100+ survey responses from small businesses across South Africa.
- 5-8 usability sessions with onboarding wizard prototypes once available.

## Timeline

- Week 1: interview recruiting and pilot.
- Week 2: 8 interviews and synthesis.
- Week 3: survey launch and analysis.
- Week 4: prototype usability test and prioritisation.

## Key questions to answer

- What percentage of messages are repeat FAQs?
- What would they never let an AI say or do?
- How much do they currently pay for receptionist or social media support?
- Do they already have a WhatsApp Business number or API access?
- Which languages and customer journey segments matter?
- What are their POPIA or privacy concerns?

## Hypotheses to validate

1. SMEs are willing to pay roughly R499-R1,999 per month for AI support.
2. A 15-minute onboarding flow is realistic without an engineering team.
3. Trust is won by handoff and transparency, not raw model intelligence.

## Clarifying questions for the product owner

1. Should the initial sales motion focus on a single vertical or cross-sector package?
2. Do you want to target a fully self-serve SaaS flow in phase one or a lighter assisted onboarding model?
3. Is a local South African support team required in the first launch cohort?
4. Which channels are mandatory for the first pilot: WhatsApp only, website widget only, or both?
5. Do you want the first tenant limit to be a small pilot or a broader beta launch?
