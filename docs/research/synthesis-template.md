## Jobs-to-be-done

- When a customer asks a common question after hours, I want a fast, accurate answer so I do not lose the lead.
- When a customer is upset or asks for a refund, I want a clear handoff so the business remains consistent and compliant.

## Journey map: customer asks a question at 9pm

1. Customer sends a WhatsApp message or visits the website widget.
2. The system identifies the business and customer context.
3. AI answers based on approved knowledge or asks a clarifying question.
4. If confidence is low or sentiment is negative, the system requests a human handoff.
5. Staff receives the conversation summary and the customer receives a helpful response.
