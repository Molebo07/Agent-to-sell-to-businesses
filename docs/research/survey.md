# Survey instrument

The goal is to validate demand, pricing sensitivity, and trust expectations.

## Survey questions

1. How many customer conversations do you receive each month?
2. Which channels do you use for customer support today?
3. Do you currently use the WhatsApp Business app or API?
4. How much do you currently spend on customer support and admin response work per month?
5. What percentage of customer messages are routine FAQ questions?
6. Which languages do your customers use most often?
7. Would you trust an AI agent to answer customer questions on WhatsApp or on your website?
8. What must never be answered by AI?
9. What monthly budget range is realistic for a subscription tool in ZAR?
10. What is your biggest problem with customer response time?
11. What are your top privacy or compliance concerns?
12. If a customer asks to speak to a human, how should the system behave?

## Success criteria

- 100+ responses target.
- Clear evidence of willingness to pay in the R499-R1,999 range.
- Strong demand for human escalation and trust controls.

## Survey hypothesis

SMEs are willing to pay for a service that reduces missed enquiries, provides fast answers, and preserves a human handoff for risky or high-value interactions.
