# Interview guide

## Structure

### 1. Warm-up (5 min)

- Introduction, verifiable consent, and what the conversation will cover.
- Explain that the goal is to understand their current support process, not to judge it.

### 2. Context (10 min)

- How do you currently answer customer messages?
- Which channels do you use today: WhatsApp, Facebook, email, call centre?
- Who handles customer questions now?

### 3. Deep dive (20 min)

- Tell me about the worst recent customer messaging experience.
- Tell me about the best recent customer conversation.
- What must the AI never say or do?
- What are your biggest bottlenecks: speed, consistency, staff availability, lead conversion?

### 4. Reaction (15 min)

- Show a prototype of the AI agent and website widget.
- Ask: what feels valuable, what feels risky, what would make you trust it?
- Capture specific objections and required approvals.

### 5. Wrap-up (5 min)

- Ask about current monthly communication volume and budget.
- Confirm if they would test a pilot and what success would look like.

## Example prompts

- What percentage of your messages are repeat FAQ questions?
- What kinds of messages would you never allow an AI to answer without a human?
- How many hours per week are lost to customer WhatsApp conversations?
- What is your current cost of a receptionist or social media manager in the month?
- How do you feel about AI talking to customers on your behalf?

## Output template

- Customer profile and segment.
- Current workflow and pain points.
- Must-never-share rules.
- Willingness to pay and budget range.
- Likelihood of pilot adoption.
